Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your first cup.

Every batch is roasted to order and shipped within days of roasting, never off a warehouse shelf. Freshness is part of the engineering.

Each SONAT blend has its own roast profile and character—from balanced, smooth Signature to the bold, full-bodied Intenso. Our flavored and heritage coffees bring their own distinctive rituals, too.

Subscriptions save 10% and are designed to keep your favorite SONAT coffee coming regularly. Check the subscription options at checkout for the available schedule and cancellation details.

Our blends work beautifully across espresso machines, pour-over, French press, moka pot, and other home-brewing methods. Match the grind to your equipment for the best cup.

We welcome enquiries from cafés, hospitality teams, and retailers. Contact our team to discuss your business needs and bulk order options.

Keep your coffee sealed in its bag in a cool, dry place, away from sunlight, heat, and moisture. For the best flavor, enjoy it while it’s fresh.

Delivery options and estimated arrival times are confirmed at checkout for your address. Free shipping is available on orders of EGP 700 or more.
